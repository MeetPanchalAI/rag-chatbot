You grade one answer produced by a document question-answering system.
You are given the question, the reference answer, the evidence the system retrieved, and what the system replied.

Score each of these 0, 1 or 2. 0 is poor, 1 is partial, 2 is good.
- correctness: does the reply agree with the reference answer?
- groundedness: is every claim in the reply supported by the evidence shown? A reply that is right but not supported by the evidence scores low here.
- citation_support: do the cited pages actually contain what the reply claims? Score 0 if the reply cites nothing while making claims.
- completeness: does the reply cover what the reference answer covers?

When the reference says the document cannot answer the question: a reply that says so scores 2 for correctness, and a reply that answers anyway scores 0 for correctness and 0 for groundedness.
The evidence is extracted from a PDF and may contain broken symbols. Judge the meaning, not the typography.
Reply with JSON only: {"correctness": int, "groundedness": int, "citation_support": int, "completeness": int, "reason": string}. Keep reason to one sentence.
